## What this sample shows

This sample report shows how public hiring activity can be converted into useful B2B sales intelligence.

The idea is simple:

Companies often reveal business priorities through the roles they are trying to hire.

A company hiring for AI, cloud security, DevSecOps, security compliance, or AI infrastructure roles may be dealing with:

- urgent project delivery
- shortage of specialist talent
- cloud or security pressure
- enterprise compliance requirements
- AI adoption moving from experiment to production
- internal teams stretched beyond current capacity

That signal may be useful for:

- IT services firms
- managed services providers
- cybersecurity service providers
- cloud consulting firms
- staffing and recruitment firms
- AI implementation and training companies

## Important note

A job post is not proof that a company wants to buy services.

It is a signal.

The value of this report is in interpreting the signal and using it to start a timely, relevant, respectful conversation.

### 1. JumpCloud

Source:

https://jobs.lever.co/jumpcloud/fec0f9d7-adcd-4be5-8659-cfd1b64e724e

Hiring signal:

Senior Software Engineer, Security Engineering in India.

Why this may matter:

The role combines identity security, data engineering, threat detection, real-time risk analysis, automated response, distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure.

This may indicate that the company is strengthening security engineering and active defense capabilities in India.

Possible service angle:

For cybersecurity or cloud service providers:

Security engineering support, identity threat detection workflows, cloud security controls, and SOC-ready automation.

For staffing firms:

Senior security engineers who combine cloud, identity, data pipelines, and threat detection.

Example outreach message:

Hi [Name],

I noticed JumpCloud is expanding Security Engineering in India, especially around identity threat detection, risk signals, and automated response.

That kind of work usually needs a mix of cloud, security, and data engineering skills that can be hard to hire quickly.

We support teams with senior security engineering capability and practical cloud/security automation support.

Would it be useful to compare notes on where you are seeing the biggest hiring or delivery bottleneck?

## Best use of this report

Use the hiring signal as context, not as a trick.

Good outreach sounds like:

"We noticed you are expanding in [specific area]. That often creates pressure around [specific issue]. We help teams with [specific support]. Would it be useful to compare notes?"

Poor outreach sounds like:

"We saw you are hiring. Do you want to buy from us?"
