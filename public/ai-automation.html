05 · Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before the call.

What kind of AI automation do you build? +

Production-grade Make.com pipelines — B2B sales intelligence, support triage, document processing, HR screening, self-healing agents, voice AI integrations, and agentic systems with feedback loops. Every build includes full error handling, documentation, and handover notes.

Are you available for freelance and international projects? +

Yes. Based in Dwarka, New Delhi, India. Delivering globally — clients across India, UK, USA, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. All project work is fully remote with clear async communication. Book a discovery call to discuss your project and timeline.

What platforms and tools do you work with? +

Make.com as the primary orchestration layer. Integrations include OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, HubSpot, Apollo, Clay, ZeroBounce, NeverBounce, Instantly, Lemlist, Slack, Bolna AI, Perplexity AI, and custom HTTP API connections. I adapt to the client's existing stack where possible.

How long does a typical automation project take? +

Simple automations (3–5 modules, single workflow) can be delivered in days. Complex pipelines — multi-step API chains, conditional routing, error recovery, and CRM integration — typically take 2 to 4 weeks to scope, build, test, and hand over. Book a call for a scoped estimate specific to your requirements.

What is the difference between Make.com automation and agentic AI? +

Make.com automation follows pre-defined rules and conditional logic. Agentic AI goes further — the AI makes contextual decisions, detects its own failures, rewrites its own prompts, and takes autonomous action without a human intervening. I build both, and have delivered self-healing and self-evolving agents that combine both approaches.

Do you provide documentation and handover support? +

Yes, on every engagement. Each scenario ships with inline module documentation, clearly labelled API swap points, and handover notes your team can follow. Training sessions are available as an add-on. The goal is always that your team can maintain and extend the build independently after delivery.
